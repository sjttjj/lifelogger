Portrait Month View Options

The core issue is that a real 7-column month grid gives each day only about 32px of width on a portrait phone. These are three ways to keep a monthly feel while making reminder titles readable.

A. Month + Weekly Agenda

Keep the 7-day month overview, but use it as date context only. Under each week, show readable agenda rows for that week.

C. Two-Column Month Blocks

Show the month as larger day cards in two columns. It keeps date scanning and lets text breathe, but you see fewer days per screen.
