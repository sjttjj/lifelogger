My Understanding

Instead of separate Month, Week, and List screens, this becomes one calendar view with a readable list and a compact visual month map.

  • The list scrolls normally, and the bottom map floats over it like a persistent mini calendar.
  • Each reminder gets a stable colour in the list and in the month map.
  • Multi-day reminders fill multiple squares with the same colour.
  • Multiple reminders on the same day show split/striped colour blocks.
  • Tapping a coloured square jumps/scrolls the list to that day or item.
